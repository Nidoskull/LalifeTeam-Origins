# Play as a Warframe, and craft others to expand your arsenal.

## Overview
- (Almost) All Warframes have to manage their Energy level properly; as without Energy, they cannot cast their abilities.
- (Almost) All Warframes have a secondary, recovering shield to better deflect damage.
- A new method of movement lets you parkour all over the place. Wall-running, bullet jumping, dodge rolling, double jumping, you've got it all, baby! And that's not even taking into account the frame-specific movement abilities!
- Though others may be able to wield Warframe abilities by wearing parts of a Warframe, you are the only Origin with the ability to gain the Passive ability from a Warframe Set.
- Maybe there's more to playing as a Warframe...?

When you choose to play as a Warframe, you cannot remove your armor in any way; *unless* you are within a particular multi-block structure, known as an Arsenal. You can only wear Warframe armor; addtionally, leaving the Arsenal without wearing a full Warframe set will forcibly equip the rest of the armor set, or if you're wearing nothing at all, forcibly re-equip your last equipped Warframe.

Modify your Warframe's stats at a different multiblock, the Mod Terminal. Equip Mods to your Warframe and your Exalted Weapons.

Change your Warframe's abilities by communicating with a Helminth. Botany has never been quite so morbid.

Obtain Warframe blueprints from gameplay and/or advancements, and craft them in the Foundry. Once their recipes are unlocked, they are never forgotten.

Choose your starting Warframe:

### Excalibur
A master of gun and blade, Excalibur is a formidable choice no matter your style.

- Slash Dash: With an Excalibur Helmet equipped, launch yourself towards enemies and slash with your Exalted Blade.
- Radial Blind: With an Excalibur Chassis equipped, emit a bright flash of light, blinding all foes within a few blocks.
- Radial Javelin: With Excalibur Systems equipped, launch ethereal javelins at your foes all around you, pinning them against walls.
- Exalted Blade: With Excalibur Hydraulics equipped, channel your energy into an ethereal blade. 
- (Passive) Radiant Swordsman: When you're wearing every piece of Excalibur, deal more damage with and attack faster with swords.

If you choose to not start with Excalibur, he can be crafted through assembling the following pieces in a Foundry.
- Helmet: Wooden Sword, Diamond Sword, 4 Scute, 8 Spider Eye, 128 Sticks (2stx)
- Chassis: Stone Sword, 64 Glass (1stx), 64 Iron Ingots (1stx), 32 Redstone Dust
- Systems: Iron Sword, 4 Redstone Comparator, 64 Glass (1stx), 64 Copper Ingots (1stx), 128 Dried Kelp (2stx)
- Hydraulics: Gold Sword, Iron Sword, 64 Iron Ingots (1stx), 32 Potatos, 128 Sticks (2stx)
 
### Mag
A force to be reckoned with, Mag is a team player. She's a worthy addition to any squad.

- Pull: With a Mag Helmet equipped, stun and yoink enemies to be directly in front of you, dealing them damage all the while.
- Magnetize: With a Mag Chassis equipped, TAP the button to put a target into a magnetic field, magnetizing sharp things towards them. HOLD the button to instead magnetize yourself, and reflect ranged damage in a destructive cone.
- Polarize: With Mag Systems equipped, emit an energy pulse that depletes shields and armor to restore that of you and allies, with leftover debris turning into Polarize Shards. Nearby Polarize Shards orbit you and cut enemies.
- Crush: With Mag Hydraulics equipped, magnetize the bones of nearby enemies, causing them to collapse on themselves.
- (Passive) Attractive: When you're wearing every piece of Mag, you have an increased pickup radius. 

### Volt
A versatile warrior, Volt's arsenal is powerful and electrifying. Handle him with care.

- Shock: With a Volt Helmet equipped, stun and deal high damage to a target. This damage chains to nearby foes.
- Speed: With a Volt Chassis equipped, energize your body and that of nearby allies, giving increased movement and attack speed for a short time.
- Electric Shield: With Volt Systems equipped, place a barrier of electricity that blocks enemy fire, and deal additional damage when firing through the shield. Pick up the shield to take it with you, though it'll be much smaller!
- Discharge: With Volt Hydraulics equipped, zap a large area around you, paralyzing and damaging everyone around you.
- (Passive) Charged: When you're wearing every piece of Volt, grounded movement will generate an electrical charge which will let you deal up to -balanced number- extra damage on your next attack.
